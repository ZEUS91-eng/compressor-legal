What the app does, and your files

The app re-encodes videos you select to make them smaller, finds similar photos, duplicates and screenshots, and shows how much cache other apps keep. Compression keeps the resolution, frame rate, aspect ratio and audio of a video, but re-encoding is a lossy process: the smaller copy is made to look the same on a phone screen, not to be bit-for-bit identical. Results, savings and speed depend on your phone's hardware and on the video itself.
