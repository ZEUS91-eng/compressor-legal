Privacy Policy for Video Compressor & Cleaner

Video Compressor & Cleaner  ·  Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Video Compressor & Cleaner ("the app"), an Android app published by The Zeus Labs ("we", "us").

The short version. The app works on your photos and videos entirely on your phone. Nothing you record, photograph or store is uploaded, copied to a server, or seen by us. We have no servers and no accounts. The only outside parties are Google AdMob, which serves the ads, and Google Play, which handles any purchase you choose to make.

Access to your photos and videos

The app's purpose is to free up storage, so it asks for permission to read the photos and videos on your device. On Android 14 and later you can choose to share only some of them. With this access the app:

  • Reads each video's size, length, resolution and format to estimate how much space compression would save
  • Compresses the videos you select, on your phone, and saves the smaller copy next to the original with the same date
  • Makes small fingerprints of your photos (a tiny greyscale summary and a sharpness score) to find similar shots and exact duplicates
  • Lists screenshots so you can review them

All of this happens on the device. Your photos, videos and their fingerprints never leave your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Deleting and moving to trash

The app never deletes anything on its own. Removing an original video or a photo always needs your confirmation, first in the app and then in Android's own dialog. On Android 11 and later, removed items go to your phone's trash, where they stay for 30 days and can be restored from your Gallery or Files app.

Usage access (app caches screen)

If you open the App caches screen, the app asks you to turn on Android's "Usage access" for it. The app uses this only to read how much cache each installed app keeps, so it can list them by size. It does not read which apps you use, when or for how long, and it stores and sends none of that. The app cannot clear other apps' data itself; it opens each app's Android settings page so you can clear its cache there. You can turn Usage access off at any time in Android Settings.

Notifications and background work

While a compression runs, the app shows a progress notification so it can keep working when you leave the app, and tells you when it is done. If you turn on Auto-Optimize (Pro), new videos are compressed while your phone is charging. Originals are still only removed after you confirm.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your settings (for example keep originals, compatible output, Auto-Optimize)
  • The total space you have saved, and counters that decide when an ad is shown
  • Cached video details and photo fingerprints, so later scans are faster
  • Whether you have Pro

This data is never sent to us. You can clear it through Android Settings, under Apps, then Video Compressor & Cleaner, then Storage, then Clear data. If Android's backup feature is on, your settings may be backed up to your own Google account, where we cannot see them.

Advertising

The free version shows banner ads, full-screen ads at the moments described in the app, and optional rewarded ads, through Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • Device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • Your interactions with the ads, such as views and taps

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us. Ads never receive your photos, videos or their details. We receive only aggregate earnings reports that identify no one. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or a US state with privacy laws, the app asks for your choice about personalised ads. You can change it at any time in the app under Settings, then Privacy choices. On any device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings, under Privacy, then Ads. Pro removes all ads.

Purchases and subscriptions

Pro is offered as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, all through Google Play, which handles the payment entirely. We never see your card number, billing address or other payment details. Google Play tells the app which plan you own, and the app uses that only to switch Pro on. Google gives us order records (item, price, country, order number) for accounting and refunds. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload, view, sell or share your photos, videos or files.
  • We do not sell or rent any data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services of our own.
  • We do not build advertising profiles.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children; as described above, the app sends us nothing at all.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase. For advertising data processed by Google, use the Google links above.

Changes to this policy

If a new feature changes what this policy says, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version of the app is released.
